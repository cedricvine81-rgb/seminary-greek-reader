# Beginning Greek Curriculum Map

The keystone document for the PowerPoint ⇄ Grammar-page integration project.
It answers one question per lesson: **what does a student know at this point?**
Every replacement exercise — in the decks and on the Grammar page — must draw
only from the grammar and vocabulary rows at or above its lesson.

Sources: the *Lessons (remote)* PowerPoint decks (canonical, per instructor);
the BGVB — *A Modern Biblical Greek Vocab Builder* (`Greek_Vocab_Builder_v20221207.xlsx`),
whose frequency-ranked sections (20 words each) define the vocabulary track.

pairing in Part 2 and (b) the exercise-constraint rules in Part 3.

## Part 1 · The grammar sequence (from the decks)

| Lesson | Deck(s) | New grammar taught | Grammar-page chapter(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | The Greek Alphabet | Alphabet, breathings, capitals, diphthongs, iota subscript, accents (recognition only), punctuation. Koine history intro. | Pronunciation |
| **2** | Grammar Review | English grammar concepts: parts of speech, gender/number/case as ideas. **No new Greek forms.** | (the English-first primers throughout) |
| **3** | 2nd Declension · 1st Declension · Adjectives · Prepositions · Pronouns (5 decks) | 2nd- and 1st-declension nouns, all four cases + article; εἰμί present; adjective agreement, attributive vs. predicate; one/two/three-case prepositions; αὐτός, ἐγώ/σύ, possessives; οὗτος/ἐκεῖνος recognition | Nouns/Adj. · Prepositions · Pronouns · Demonstratives |
| **4** | 3rd Declension | 3rd-declension endings and stem types (incl. πατήρ-type, -εως nouns, contracting nouns); πᾶς; εἷς/οὐδείς; double negatives | Nouns/Adj. (3rd decl.) · Pronouns (οὐδείς) |
| **5** | Basic Verb Forms (+ Deponent Verbs handout) | Present & imperfect indicative, active and middle/passive (Minimum 3); augment rules (vowel lengthening, compounds); contraction & elision basics; **deponents** | Indicatives · Deponents · (Contract Verbs, first contact) |
| **6** | Tense Identifiers · 2nd Aorists and Liquid Verbs (2 decks) | Future, 1st aorist (act/mid/pass), perfect (Minimums 4–5); consonant + σ; **2nd aorists**; **liquid futures & aorists** | Indicatives (tense machine) · 2nd Aorists · Liquid Verbs |
| **7** | Forming Participles · Using Participles (2 decks) | All participle formation (Minimum 6); Simultaneous/Sequence; adverbial / attributive / substantival; decision process; (awareness: causal, concessive, instrumental uses) | Participles |
| **8** | Subjunctive · Imperative and Infinitive (2 decks) | Subjunctive forms + flag words, hortatory, deliberative, prohibition, emphatic negation (Min 7-A); imperative −τω/−τωσαν (Min 7-B); infinitives λύειν/λῦσαι, δεῖ + acc + inf | Subjunctives · Imperatives · Infinitives |
| **9** | Mi Verbs | μι-verb system (Minimum 8): two stems, present & aorist of δίδωμι/τίθημι/ἵστημι; ἀφίημι | μι-Verbs · Principal Parts (first contact) |
| **10** | Basic Syntax | Conditionals (3 classes); genitive absolute; comparison of adjectives & adverbs; optative (awareness only) | Conditionals · Participles (gen. abs.) · Conj. & Adv. |

Cumulative "can parse" inventory (what exercises may *require*, not merely gloss):

- **After L3:** 1st/2nd-decl. nouns & adjectives in all cases; the article; εἰμί (present); personal pronouns; preposition + case readings.
- **After L4:** + all 3rd-declension nouns; πᾶς; οὐδείς.
- **After L5:** + present & imperfect indicative in all voices; deponents; -έω contract presents.
- **After L6:** + future, aorist (1st & 2nd), perfect indicative; liquid futures/aorists.
- **After L7:** + participles (all the above tenses).
- **After L8:** + subjunctives, imperatives, infinitives.
- **After L9:** + μι-verbs.
- **After L10:** + conditional classification; genitive absolutes.

Working rule for exercise vocabulary: **at grammar lesson N, use only words
from the cumulative rank range above**, plus the closed-class "grammar words"
the decks themselves introduce (article forms, εἰμί, the pronoun paradigms,
the prepositions taught in L3).

## Part 3 · Exercise-writing rules

1. **Vocabulary**: only words within the cumulative BGVB range for the lesson
   (Part 2), plus deck-introduced grammar words. Any word outside the range
   must be glossed inline — used sparingly, never as the point being tested.
2. **Forms**: only forms from the cumulative inventory (Part 1). Before L5,
   verbs appear only as glossed 3rd-person presents (the decks' own practice);
   before L7, no participles; etc.
3. **Register**: original compositions or NT phrases/verses. Never reuse
   Duff's exercise sentences (copyright — see docs/copyright-notes.md).
   NT quotations: Greek text is public domain; translations are our own.
4. **Pedagogical cycle** (both media): ① concept in English → ② Greek
   examples → ③ guided "we do" (prompted steps; in the app the GuidedExample
   component, in decks prompt-slide + answer-slide pairs) → ④ independent
   "you do" with answers for review.
5. **Deck ⇄ chapter mirroring**: each lesson's exercises exist in both the
   .pptx and the matching Grammar chapter; Biblical examples used in decks
   also appear in the chapter with Reader links.
